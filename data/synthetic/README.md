# Synthetic Campaign & Engagement Directory

> [!WARNING]
> The UCI Online Retail II dataset is purely transactional and does NOT contain marketing campaign responses or CRM campaign data.
> The datasets in this folder are explicitly **synthetic simulation layers** created strictly for evaluating scenario planners and Next-Best-Action logic. They are NEVER treated as real-world marketing response truths.

## Synthetic Data Artifacts
- `synthetic_campaign_history.csv`: Simulated past campaign touches, offers, costs, and responses based on parameterized response models.
- `scenario_definitions.yaml`: Pre-configured business scenarios (e.g. Win-back high-risk, upsell loyalists).
